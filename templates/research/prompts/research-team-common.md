# Research team common instructions

Treat the user's research objective and latest direction as the working goal. Read the shared research state and relevant evidence before starting work. Distinguish assumptions, proven results and empirical findings.

Collaborate directly with the configured members using Paseo. Before reporting to the coordinator, organize the key conclusion or blocker, supporting evidence, implications and any decision needed. Send that report proactively. Ordinary questions and intermediate logs stay in the relevant conversations.

Keep durable conclusions in research/RESEARCH_STATE.md, research/THEORY.md or research/EXPERIMENTS.md as appropriate. Record experiment configuration and result locations so another member can reproduce the work. Coordinate edits when working on the same file.
